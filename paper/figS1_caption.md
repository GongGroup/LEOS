## Caption

**Fig. 3** Comparison with the atomic-overlap-matrix analysis of ref. X on its
40 test solids. **(a)** Element-mean oxidation states from ref. X (abscissa)
and from this work (ordinate), one point per element per compound (88 points
from 39 solids); the marker area is proportional to the number of coincident
points. Filled symbols: both methods were applied to the same crystal
structure. Open symbols, and the open sector of a marker where both kinds
coincide: the cell computed here differs from, or could not be matched to, the
structure analysed in ref. X (K, CsH<sub>3</sub> and the three HgF<sub>4</sub>
cells). Horizontal bars: XeO<sub>3</sub> at 150 GPa, for which ref. X gives
site-resolved states (Xe +6 and +4) without site multiplicities, so that only
an interval for the element mean follows. Every off-diagonal point is labelled
with compound and element; HgF<sub>4</sub>* is the HgF<sub>4</sub> cell that
contains F<sub>2</sub> units. LiN<sub>5</sub> is not shown because ref. X
reports no assignment for it. In SrBe(OH)<sub>4</sub> the hydroxide group
enters as one unit, as in ref. X. **(b)** Spread of every Wannier function (WF)
against the distance of its centre from the nearest nucleus, for Ca<sub>3</sub>AuN
and high-pressure K (*oP*8), each below a conventional ionic reference from the
same benchmark (Cs<sub>3</sub>AuO and KMgF<sub>3</sub>); *n* is the number of
WFs. Dashed line: 3 Å<sup>2</sup>, the spread above which a WF is flagged as
unlocalised. **(c)** Number of solids per chemical class for which all element
means agree, at least one differs, or the two results cannot be compared
(different or unmatched crystal structure, or no assignment in ref. X).

- Computed by `figS1_comparison.py` with `pyleos.parsers.load_case` and
  `pyleos.transport.assign_case` at the defaults (β = 10, Allen
  electronegativity, geometric + electronegativity cost) from POSCAR, EOSCAR
  and, where present, wannier90.wout in:
  - `cases/<case>/v3/` (role `comas` in `cases/INDEX.tsv`) for 30 solids: ba3si4, cah4, ceh4, cs2pt, csf3,
    csf5, csh3, hgf4_a, hgf4_b, hgf4_c, k_hp, lin5, mgh4, mgxe, sch4, srh4,
    thh4, xef2, xef3, xef4, xeo_a, xeo_b, xeo2_a, xeo2_b, xeo3_a, xeo3_b,
    ybafe2o5_a, ybafe2o5_b, yh4, zrh4;
  - `cases/<case>/v3/` for cro3, ca3aun (benchmark set `wave1`) and kmgf3,
    nabf4, srbe_oh_4, cs3auo, rb3auo, sega (GaSe), wcl4, rb2cucl4 (set
    `wave2`), the 10 solids shared with the 74-case benchmark.
- Cache: `_cache/fig3_leos.json`, keyed by the SHA-1 of the input files;
  `python3 figS1_comparison.py --recompute` rebuilds it (about 25 s).
- Cross-checks run by the script: the 21 element means and the 12 stray counts
  quoted in docs/RESULTS.md, section 4, are reproduced; n_stray and n_ghost of
  the 10 shared solids equal `results/bench74_golden.tsv` (9 OK, ca3aun FAIL
  with dev 2.00 because the benchmark reference is Au −3).
- Panel (b): spreads and centres from the wannier90.wout of k_hp and from the
  `#WF` block of the EOSCAR of ca3aun, cs3auo and kmgf3; distance = minimum-image
  distance of the WF centre from the nearest atom (`WfInfo.d1`); the 3 Å<sup>2</sup>
  line is `pyleos.transport.STRAY_SPREAD`.

### Ref. X (abscissa of (a))

- Typed once, in the table `LIT` of the script, from
  docs/comas_vila_2025_notes.md, lines 341–365 (journal pages 7078–7084); each
  row carries its line number and page, and each value was re-read in the PDF
  under docs/refs/.
- Stated as such in ref. X: Cr; K, Mg, F; Na, B, F; Sr, Be, OH; Ga, Se; Ba and
  the Si<sub>4</sub> fragment; Pt; W, Cl; Cu, Cl, Rb; Fe in both
  YBaFe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>5</sub> phases; M and H in the MH<sub>4</sub> series;
  Xe and O in XeO and XeO<sub>2</sub>; Xe, O1, O2 in XeO<sub>3</sub> at 130 GPa;
  Xe in the fluorides; Mg, Xe; Hg; the H<sub>3</sub>, F<sub>3</sub> and
  F<sub>5</sub> fragments; Au in Ca<sub>3</sub>AuN; K.
- Derived from stated values:
  - by charge neutrality: O in CrO<sub>3</sub>; Cs in Cs<sub>2</sub>Pt,
    CsH<sub>3</sub> and CsF<sub>3</sub>; N in
    Ca<sub>3</sub>AuN (Ca +2 follows from its unoccupied frontier orbital); F
    in the Xe fluorides and in HgF<sub>4</sub>; the 1 : 1 site ratios in XeO at
    200 GPa and in XeF<sub>3</sub>. (For XeO at 200 GPa the summary formula
    printed on p. 7082 reads Xe1(+2); the discussion on the same page and
    charge neutrality give Xe1 +4, as transcribed in the notes. The element
    mean, +2, is the same either way.);
  - fragment charge divided by the number of atoms: Si −6/4, H and F −1/3,
    F −1/5;
  - site average over the cell described in ref. X (2 M + 4 H<sub>b</sub> +
    4 H<sub>a</sub>): H −1/2 for M = Mg, Ca, Sr and −3/4 for M = Sc, Y;
    O −4/3 in XeO<sub>3</sub> at 130 GPa (two O1 at −2, one O2 at 0);
  - "consistent with the IUPAC report" in ref. X, IUPAC values taken: Cs, Rb,
    Au, O in the two aurides; Y, Ba, O in YBaFe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>5</sub>.
- Not available, not plotted as points:
  - LiN<sub>5</sub>: no assignment in ref. X (R = 50 %); left out of (a),
    counted as not comparable in (c). This work gives Li +1, N −0.2.
  - XeO<sub>3</sub> at 150 GPa: Xe1 +6, Xe2 +4 and one neutral O site are given
    without multiplicities, so the element means are not defined by the text.
    Drawn as intervals: Xe between +4 and +6, O between −2 and −4/3 (the latter
    by charge neutrality). The site table of the SI would turn both into
    points.
  - O and H of SrBe(OH)<sub>4</sub> separately: ref. X assigns the OH fragment
    (−1); it is compared with the sum of O and H found here (−2 + 1).
  - The two non-nuclear attractors of K (−2 each in ref. X) have no
    counterpart here.

### Comparisons that are not on the same structure (open symbols in (a), "not comparable" in (c))

- **K.** Ref. X analyses a cell with 4 K atoms and 2 non-nuclear attractors
  (p. 7079); `cases/k_hp/v3` is *oP*8 K with 8 atoms. This mismatch is
  not flagged in docs/. Independently of the phase, an elemental solid has
  element mean 0 in this work by construction.
- **HgF<sub>4</sub>, all three cells.** Ref. X describes three structures of
  the same symmetry (*I*4/*m*) and bonding pattern at 50, 100 and 200 GPa. The
  three cells computed here have different bonding patterns (read from the
  POSCAR files by the script): cell a, square-planar Hg (4 × 1.96 Å); cell b,
  six-coordinate Hg and F<sub>2</sub> units (F–F 1.43 Å); cell c, cubic, 5
  atoms, six-coordinate Hg. docs/comas_vila_2025_notes.md records the
  correspondence as unverified; solids 34, 35, 36 of ref. X are paired with
  cells a, b, c only by directory order. Cells a and c give Hg +4 as in
  ref. X; cell b gives Hg +3.
- **CsH<sub>3</sub>.** Linear H<sub>3</sub> units in ref. X; two isolated
  triangular H<sub>3</sub> units here (docs/testset_findings.md).
- **LiN<sub>5</sub>.** Pentazolate ring in ref. X; N<sub>3</sub> + N<sub>2</sub>
  here (docs/testset_findings.md).

Same phase, approximate lattice (filled symbols, counted as agree):
Cs<sub>2</sub>Pt (c/a 16.6 % below experiment, docs/testset_registry.tsv) and
MgXe (lattice parameter from an equation of state, ±1.5 %, docs/RESULTS.md).

To reclassify a solid, change its `struct` entry in `LIT`
(`same` / `lattice` → filled and comparable; `different` / `unmatched` → open
and not comparable).

### Scope of the comparison

Panel (a) compares element means only. Site-resolved patterns differ in
several solids whose means agree: XeO at 200 GPa (+6, +2, 0, 0 here; +4 and 0
in ref. X), both XeO<sub>2</sub> phases (+2 to +6 here; +4 in ref. X), the
mixed-valence YBaFe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>5</sub> phase (+1 and +4 here; +2.5 in
ref. X), ScH<sub>4</sub> and YH<sub>4</sub> (three H<sup>−</sup> and one
H<sup>0</sup> here; two H<sup>−</sup> and two H<sup>−1/2</sup> in ref. X), GaSe
(+1 and +3 here; +2 in ref. X) and Rb<sub>2</sub>CuCl<sub>4</sub> (+1 and +3
here; +2 in ref. X). Ref. X reports no timings; none are compared.
